# Where Marnie's next collectors could find her

## A practical launch shortlist · September 12, 2026

**Start with Instagram, Pinterest and one buyer marketplace.** Lead with a real mask–smoke–reveal sequence, then show the finished lighthouse and egret clearly. The unusual process earns attention; the subject, physical quality and trustworthy buying details give someone a reason to become a collector.

The recommendations below are editorial judgments based on the artwork and each channel's published purpose. They are not forecasts of demand. No accounts were created, posts published, applications submitted or organizations contacted. Location, sellable inventory, prices, dimensions and existing social accounts remain unconfirmed.

### How to read the shortlist

**NOW** means suitable for a small initial test. **NEXT** means prepare after the photographs and offer are ready. **LATER** means conditional, selective or costly. Effort is my estimate, excluding making new artwork: **low** under an hour per item; **medium** several hours of preparation; **high** portfolio/application work or an event commitment. Organic channels below assume no paid advertising; paid promotion is not part of the proposed first month.

## What I would do in the first 30 days

| Window | Action | What would count as useful evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 1–5: make the work easy to trust** | Confirm physical dimensions, availability, framing, shipping region and price floor. Photograph both works straight-on, detail and actual scale. Record one authentic process video. Confirm the studio-list form reaches the intended inbox. Prepare a simple source log. | Two accurate artwork records and working visitor-to-inquiry/list signup path. Pixel dimensions are not physical artwork dimensions. |
| **Days 6–12: run a small discovery test** | Publish, once separately authorized, two Instagram posts/Reels and four Pinterest Pins using existing original assets. Reuse the better piece on the existing Facebook Page if there is one. Keep one clear invitation: see the work and join the studio list. | Saves, gallery visits, opt-ins and specific questions about a piece. Record the source and effort. Do not treat likes as purchase intent. |
| **Days 13–20: put a real offer in front of buyers** | If a sellable original is ready, build one Saatchi listing or prepare the Artfinder application—not both by default. If no inventory is ready, continue the waitlist honestly. Prepare a Colossal pitch; use one current r/Art promo thread if desired. | Qualified inquiries about availability, dimensions, shipping or price. A transaction is stronger evidence than an inquiry. |
| **Days 21–30: choose what to repeat** | Compare formats and sources. Repeat the strongest original content angle, and consider a single local chapter feature inquiry after location and permission are resolved. Defer fairs and subscriptions without a costed plan. | Compare qualified inquiries per hour of effort, opt-ins per gallery visit, and actual sales where available. Keep experiments with promising evidence; one low-traffic month is not proof there is no market. |

This is a proposed sequence, not a posting authorization or a scheduled automation. Avoid paid traffic until the destination and offer are credible. If prints are pursued, first approve a physical proof and edition policy; do not invent scarcity to improve response.

## Boundaries and open questions

No private Facebook-group rules were accessible or assumed. Birding groups, collector groups and other artists' comment sections are not automatic promotional surfaces. Audience fit is a hypothesis; platform membership counts and vendor success stories were intentionally not used as proof of likely sales. The most useful next inputs are Marnie's city/country, existing social profiles, number of available originals, actual sizes and preferred selling/shipping scope.
